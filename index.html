Our Story

A slow-coffee corner of Fort Kochi

Arasta was born from a simple idea: Fort Kochi deserves a place where good coffee, good light and good company slow the day down. Every cup is brewed with care, every corner is built for lingering.

From sunrise filter coffee to unhurried evening conversations, Arasta is a small, honest space shaped by this city's old streets, sea breeze and stories.
